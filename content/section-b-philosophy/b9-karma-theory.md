# B.9. Karma Theory

### B.9.1. Introduction
The soul is like a magnet and karma is like iron particles. Our Kashäy (passions) attract these particles. Our goal is to get rid of previously attracted karma and stop new accumulation.

### B.9.2. Classification of Karma
Karma is divided into two main categories:
1. **Ghäti Karma (Destructive):** These affect the true nature of the soul.
    * **Jnänävarniya:** Obscures knowledge.
    * **Darshanävarniya:** Obscures perception.
    * **Mohaniya:** Obscures right conduct and belief (the most dangerous).
    * **Antaräya:** Creates obstacles.
2. **Aghäti Karma (Non-destructive):** These affect the physical conditions of the soul.
    * **Vedaniya:** Causes feelings of pleasure/pain.
    * **Näm:** Determines physical body/features.
    * **Gotra:** Determines status/family.
    * **Äyushya:** Determines life span.
