# B.7. Nav Tattva Part II: Punya and Päp

### B.7.2. Punya (Virtue)
Punya refers to virtuous or wholesome activities of the mind, speech, and body that lead to happiness and comfort in future lives. It is acquired through compassion, charity, and honesty.

### B.7.3. Päp (Sin)
Päp refers to non-virtuous or sinful activities that lead to suffering and misery. Jainism identifies 18 types of sinful activities (**Päpsthanaks**), including:
* **Himsä:** Violence
* **Asatya:** Untruth
* **Adatädäna:** Stealing
* **Maithun:** Unchastity
* **Parigraha:** Possessiveness
* **Krodh, Mäna, Mäyä, Lobha:** Anger, Pride, Deceit, Greed

### B.7.4. Four-Fold Combinations
1. **Punya-anubandhi Punya:** Earning Punya while doing virtuous deeds.
2. **Päp-anubandhi Punya:** Earning Päp while enjoying the fruits of past Punya.
3. **Punya-anubandhi Päp:** Earning Punya while suffering the fruits of past Päp.
4. **Päp-anubandhi Päp:** Earning Päp while suffering the fruits of past Päp.
