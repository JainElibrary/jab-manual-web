# B.3. Six Universal Substances (Shad Dravya)

Jainism believes that the universe is made up of six eternal and independent substances called Dravyas. These are:
1. **Jiva (Living Being):** The substance that has consciousness.
2. **Pudgala (Matter):** The substance that has form, color, taste, smell, and touch.
3. **Dharmästikäy (Medium of Motion):** The substance that helps Jiva and Pudgala to move.
4. **Adharmästikäy (Medium of Rest):** The substance that helps Jiva and Pudgala to rest.
5. **Äkäshästikäy (Space):** The substance that provides space to all other substances.
6. **Käl (Time):** The substance that is responsible for the change and modifications of all other substances.

Except for Käl, the other five substances are called **Ästikäy** because they occupy more than one space-point (Pradesh). Käl occupies only one space-point and is therefore not an Ästikäy.
