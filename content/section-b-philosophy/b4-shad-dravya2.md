# B.4. Shad Dravya I: Jiva

This chapter goes deeper into the "Living" substance among the six dravyas. 

### B.4.1. Prän (Life-forces)
A worldly Jiva is recognized by its **Prän** (vitality). There are 10 types of Präns:
* 5 Senses (Indriya)
* 3 Activities (Mind, Speech, Body)
* 1 Life-span (Äyushya)
* 1 Respiration (Shväshochchhväsh)

### B.4.2. Classification by Movement
Jivas are also classified by their ability to move:
* **Sthävar Jiva:** Immobile beings (One-sensed only).
* **Trasa Jiva:** Mobile beings (Two to Five-sensed).
