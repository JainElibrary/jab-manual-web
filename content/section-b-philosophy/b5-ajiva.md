# B.5. Shad Dravya II: Ajiva (Non-living Substances)

### B.5.1. Introduction to Ajiva
Everything in the universe that does not possess consciousness is classified as Ajiva. While Jiva (Living Being) has the power of knowing and feeling, Ajiva is inert. However, Jiva and Ajiva are intertwined in our worldly existence.

### B.5.2. Classification of Ajiva
Ajiva is divided into five distinct categories. Together with Jiva, these make up the Shad Dravya (Six Universal Substances).

1. **Pudgalästikäy (Matter):** Pudgala is derived from *Pud* (to combine) and *Gala* (to dissociate). Matter is that which has color, smell, taste, and touch. It exists as Paramänu (atoms) or Skandha (aggregates).
2. **Dharmästikäy (Medium of Motion):** It helps Jiva and Pudgala to move, like water helps fish to swim.
3. **Adharmästikäy (Medium of Rest):** It helps Jiva and Pudgala to rest or stay still, like the shade of a tree helps a traveler to rest.
4. **Äkäshästikäy (Space):** It provides space to all other substances. It is divided into Lokäkäsh (where dravyas exist) and Alokäkäsh (empty space beyond).
5. **Käl (Time):** It measures the change or modification of substances. Unlike others, it is not an Ästikäy as it does not occupy multiple space-points.
