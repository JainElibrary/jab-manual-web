# B.2. Characteristics of the Soul

The soul has many characteristics, but some of the most important ones are:
1. **Chetanämaya (Conscious):** The soul is the only substance that has consciousness.
2. **Amurti (Formless):** The soul has no form, no color, no taste, no smell, and no touch.
3. **Karta (Doer):** The soul is the doer of its own actions (Karmas).
4. **Bhokta (Enjoyer):** The soul is the enjoyer of the fruits of its actions.
5. **Nitya (Eternal):** The soul is eternal. It was never created and will never be destroyed.
6. **Deha-parimän (Size of the Body):** In its worldly state, the soul occupies the same space as the body it inhabits. It expands and contracts to fit the size of the body.
7. **Aguru-laghu (Constant):** The soul's number of space-points (Pradesh) remains constant. It is neither heavy nor light.
