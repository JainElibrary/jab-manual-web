# B.8. Nav Tattva Part III: Samvar, Nirjarä, and Moksh

### B.8.2. Samvar (Stoppage of Influx)
Samvar is the prevention of new karma from entering the soul. There are 57 practical ways of Samvar, including:
* **Samiti:** Carefulness in walking, speaking, and eating.
* **Gupti:** Restraint of mind, speech, and body.
* **Bhävanä:** 12 types of reflections on the nature of reality.

### B.8.3. Nirjarä (Eradication of Karma)
Nirjarä is the shedding or partial eradication of accumulated karma. It is achieved through **Tap** (Austerities).
* **External Austerities:** Fasting, limiting food, and physical penance.
* **Internal Austerities:** Repentance (Präyashchitta), Modesty (Vinay), Service (Veyävachcha), Study (Swädhyäy), and Meditation (Dhyän).

### B.8.4. Moksh (Total Liberation)
Moksh is the state where the soul is completely free from all karmic bondage. The soul attains its true nature of infinite knowledge, perception, power, and bliss.
