# Section A.1: Jain Prayers

Prayers are a way to express our gratitude toward the Tirthankars and to purify our own thoughts. In Jainism, we do not ask for worldly favors; we pray to achieve the virtues of the liberated souls.

## A.1.1 Namaskär Mahämangal Sutra (Navkär Mantra)
The Navkär Mantra is the most fundamental prayer in Jainism. We bow to the virtues of the five supreme beings (Pancha Paramesthi) rather than specific individuals.

* **Namo Arihantänam:** I bow to the Arihants (Destroyers of inner enemies).
* **Namo Siddhänam:** I bow to the Siddhas (Liberated souls).
* **Namo Äyariyänam:** I bow to the Ächäryas (Spiritual leaders).
* **Namo Uvajjhäyänam:** I bow to the Upädhyäys (Teachers).
* **Namo Loe Savva Sähunam:** I bow to all the Sädhus (Monks) in the universe.

## A.1.2 Mängalik - Chattäri Mangalam
This prayer highlights the four auspicious entities that provide us with spiritual shelter.

1. **Arihantä Mangalam:** Arihants are auspicious.
2. **Siddhä Mangalam:** Siddhas are auspicious.
3. **Sahu Mangalam:** Sädhus are auspicious.
4. **Kevali Pannatto Dhammo Mangalam:** The religion expounded by the Kevali (Omniscient) is auspicious.
